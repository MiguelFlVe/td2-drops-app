**What's new in 2.1.2**

- **First Sight:** this Named assault rifle has no unique talent, its perk only changes the base stats (+30% Headshot Damage, -50% Reload Speed). Log drop and Odds now let you pick both its attribute and its talent, like a High End assault rifle, and show the base-stat perk as fixed.
- First Sight drops logged with an older version (no talent recorded) still load and still count for attributes; when you ask Odds for a talent, they're left out and a note says how many.
