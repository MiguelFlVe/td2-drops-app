**What's new in 2.1.1**

- **Core Strength backpack:** it always has all three core attributes (Weapon Damage, Armor and Skill Tier) and no minor attributes. Log drop no longer asks for a core or an attribute, Odds shows it as a fixed roll (100%), and History and the CSV list the three cores.
- Core Strength backpacks logged with an older version are fixed automatically: their placeholder core and attribute are removed, and they no longer skew the odds of other gear.
